Postveil / privacy notice

Your mailbox stays yours.

Postveil provides custom-domain email access. This notice explains the information Postveil uses to operate the service.

Information we use

  • Account information such as your email address, sign-in method, and optional profile name.
  • Mailbox data, messages, attachments, delivery events, and settings needed to provide email service.
  • Security information such as session records, rate-limit signals, and audit events used to protect accounts.

How providers are used

Postveil uses infrastructure providers to store and deliver mail. Provider credentials are kept server-side. Postveil does not sell mailbox content or use it to train an AI model. Email delivery can require sharing message data with the selected delivery provider.

Security and retention

Access is tenant-scoped and protected with authenticated sessions. Data is retained while your account is active or as needed to complete delivery, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. You may request account deletion or export through the service.
