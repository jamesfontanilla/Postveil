Postveil / terms of service

Use mail responsibly.

These baseline terms describe the responsibilities for using Postveil while the hosted service is in early release. Have a qualified adviser review them before accepting paid customers.

Your account and domains

You must provide accurate account information, protect your sign-in methods, and control every domain and sender address connected to your account. Do not claim a domain or mailbox you do not control.

Acceptable use

Do not use Postveil for spam, phishing, malware, harassment, unlawful surveillance, unsolicited bulk mail, impersonation, or content that violates applicable law or provider policies. We may throttle, quarantine, suspend, or terminate abusive sending.

Email delivery and privacy

Email delivery depends on third-party infrastructure and recipient networks. Messages may be processed by delivery, storage, security, and identity providers. Postveil is not universal end-to-end encryption; ordinary SMTP delivery is not end-to-end encrypted.

Availability and limits

The service is provided during an early-release period and may change. Quotas, storage, sending limits, retention, and provider availability apply. Keep independent copies of important information.

Contact

For questions or abuse reports, contact jamesfontanilla.dev@gmail.com. See the privacy notice.
