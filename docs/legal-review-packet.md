# Legal review packet

The public privacy notice and terms are an early-release baseline, not legal
advice. Before a paid hosted launch, have counsel or a qualified privacy
professional finalize:

- Terms of service and acceptable-use policy
- Privacy notice and data-subject request process
- Data-processing agreement and international-transfer language
- Subprocessor list and change-notice process
- Retention, deletion, backup, and legal-hold rules
- Security-incident and breach-notification procedure
- Subscription cancellation, refund, tax, and invoice terms
- Support, abuse, and law-enforcement contact details

Do not advertise universal end-to-end encryption: ordinary SMTP delivery is
not end-to-end encrypted. If zero-access or client-side encryption is offered,
define the supported clients, key recovery model, metadata retained, and what
happens when a recipient cannot decrypt a message.
